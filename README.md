# AI Avatars in Social Work Education

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23442035.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23442035)
[![License: CC BY-NC 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY--NC_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

Open educational resource (interactive web guide; CC BY-NC 4.0) by D. L. Schuman, The University of Texas at Arlington. Developed for the UTA Center for Research on Teaching and Learning Excellence (CRTLE) Professional Learning Community, Future-Ready Teaching: Artificial Intelligence, 2025–2026. Four branching AI avatar case videos (each with an on-screen decision point and two practice paths), case-based teaching strategies, and ethics guidance for using AI avatars in social work education.

Live: https://ai-avatars-in-socialwork.netlify.app

## How to cite

Schuman, D. L. (2026). *AI avatars in social work education: Case-based teaching and ethics guidance* (Version 2.2.0) [Open educational resource]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23442035

The DOI above (concept DOI) always resolves to the newest version. Each GitHub release is archived on Zenodo with its own version DOI; v2.2.0 is https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23442036. Citation metadata is in `CITATION.cff`.
