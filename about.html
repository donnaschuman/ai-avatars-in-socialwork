How to cite

Schuman, D. L. (2026). AI avatars in social work education: Case-based teaching and ethics guidance [Companion web resource]. https://ai-avatars-in-socialwork.netlify.app/

AI use and authorship

The author developed all four cases, including the scenarios, client details, and prompts. The scripts were drafted with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) and reviewed by the author for clinical accuracy and alignment with social work values. The videos were generated with HeyGen using licensed stock avatars modeled on consenting actors and AI-generated voices. Captions were timed with automated speech alignment and checked word for word against the approved scripts. Page content was drafted with support from SciSpace (per the author's instructions) and AnswerThis (literature review assistance); design, interactive features, and video production assistance used Claude Fable 5, Claude Fable 5.1, and Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic). The author reviewed and edited all content and takes full responsibility for it.

Accessibility

Every video has closed captions, on by default, and a full transcript with speaker labels. Spanish lines in Case 4 appear in italics in the captions, with English translations in the transcript. All controls work with a keyboard, and text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects no personal information. Checklist selections exist only in your browser tab. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
